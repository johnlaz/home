Photo gallery
Front and rear photos of the home, with room for more, plus photos on each piece of equipment.
Backup and restore
One file holds everything in the app, so an owner can move to a new phone without starting over.

The scanner, help, and suggestion features use the owner's own free Groq API key, entered once in settings. Everything else works without one. All of an owner's data stays in their browser and is never uploaded.

A closing gift that keeps working

A binder gets buried in a closet. An app on the phone gets opened the day the filter needs changing.

For you

  • A handoff that stands out. Buyers leave with the keys and a complete record of their home.
  • Your name stays in the phone. You're listed as the builder, with your warranty contact one tap away.
  • Common questions answered up front. Paint codes, filter sizes, and model numbers are already in the app.
  • No accounts to manage. The owner's data lives on their own device, so there's no user database for you to run.

For your buyers

  • Every system accounted for. Model, serial, and a maintenance schedule that flags what's due.
  • Selections on record. Colors, tile, stone, and hardware, down to the product code.
  • All the paperwork together. Plans, permits, warranties, and insurance, searchable from one box.
  • Easy to start. It installs to the home screen like any app, and there's no account to create.

How we put one together

  1. 1

    You send what you have

    Plans, the selection sheet, an equipment list, and your warranty terms.

  2. 2

    We build the home's app

    Systems and schedules, finishes, documents, contacts, photos, and the 3D model if there is one.

  3. 3

    The buyer gets it at closing

    They open it on their phone and add it to the home screen.

  4. 4

    It stays current

    Owners log service, add photos, and keep their own records. You stay on the contact list.
